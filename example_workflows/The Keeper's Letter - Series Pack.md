# Series 1 - The Keeper's Letter

NOTE:
A lighthouse on a northern cape, autumn 1954. The keeper's daughter has run the light alone since winter. One morning the supply boat leaves a letter on the sill - the first in a year - and by nightfall she has to decide whether to answer it.

**C-01 The Cape at Dawn**

FRAME: landscape
NOTE:
The cape from the air at first light: the white tower, the keeper's cottage, the long path down to the landing. No one in sight yet.
Caption: Forty miles from the nearest town, the light still turns.

QUALITY · Cold Dawn, Medium Format
```
medium-format film photograph, cold blue dawn turning pale gold at the horizon, low mist over the sea, soft directional first light, fine film grain, muted natural colour, rich texture in rock, grass and weathered paint
```

SCENE · Northern Cape, Lighthouse
```
a white stone lighthouse with a black lantern room on a high green cape above a grey sea, a small whitewashed keeper's cottage beside it, a narrow footpath winding down the cliff to a wooden landing stage, waves breaking white on dark rocks below, a few gulls over the water
```

CAMERA · Drone-High Extreme Long Shot
```
landscape extreme long shot from very high above the sea like a drone, looking down at the whole cape, the lighthouse small in the upper third of the frame, the path and the landing tracing a line to the lower corner
```

ABSOLUTE · Empty Cape, Dawn
```
no people, quiet and still, the lantern still faintly lit against the brightening sky, a sense of great distance and solitude
```

**C-02 The Letter on the Sill**

FRAME: portrait
NOTE:
A close look at what the boat left behind: a cream envelope on the cottage window sill, a red wax seal, salt on the glass.
Caption: The first letter in a year.

QUALITY · Window Light, Still Life
```
medium-format film photograph, soft cool window light from the side, gentle shadows, shallow depth of field, fine film grain, true-to-life colour, crisp detail in paper fibre, wax and wood grain
```

SCENE · Cottage Window Sill
```
a deep painted wooden window sill inside the keeper's cottage, flaking white paint, a few dried sea-pinks in a small glass jar, the grey sea blurred beyond salt-streaked glass
```

CAMERA · Macro Close-Up on the Seal
```
portrait macro close-up at sill height with a 100mm macro lens at f/2.8, sharp focus on the red wax seal and the handwritten address, the jar and the window falling softly out of focus
```

ABSOLUTE · The Unopened Letter
```
a cream envelope with a red wax seal and a faded postage stamp lying on the sill, unopened, no hands and no people in the frame
```

**C-03 Reading by the Lamp**

FRAME: landscape
NOTE:
Evening in the lamp room. She has finally opened it and reads by the light of the brass oil lamp, the beam turning slowly behind her.
Caption: She read it twice before she let herself believe it.

QUALITY · Lamplight Interior
```
medium-format film photograph, warm oil-lamp light against cool blue dusk from the windows, deep soft shadows, fine film grain, rich warm and cool contrast, detailed textures of brass, wool and paper
```

SUBJECT · The Keeper's Daughter
```
a woman in her late twenties with a calm, weathered beauty, light freckles, grey-green eyes, dark auburn hair loosely pinned back with a few strands falling free, natural skin with fine texture
```

OUTFIT · Wool and Canvas
```
a thick cream fisherman's sweater with the sleeves pushed up, dark wool trousers, a faded canvas work apron, a thin silver chain at her neck
```

POSE · Reading, Lips Parted
```
seated on a low wooden stool, holding the opened letter in both hands close to the lamp, head bowed over the page, lips slightly parted, eyes lowered to the words, a stillness of complete attention
```

SCENE · Lamp Room at Dusk
```
the round lamp room at the top of the lighthouse, curved iron-framed windows, the great glass lens glowing faintly behind her, a brass oil lamp on a small table, a logbook and a tin mug beside it, the dusk sea dark blue beyond the glass
```

CAMERA · Three-Quarter, Eye Level
```
landscape three-quarter shot at her seated eye level with a 50mm lens at f/2, her face and the letter sharp in the lamplight, the lens and windows soft behind her
```

ABSOLUTE · The Letter in the Light
```
the letter catches the lamplight so the handwriting shows faintly through the paper, the only warm light in a cold blue room
```

SUBJECT · The Keeper's Daughter

a woman in her late twenties with a calm, weathered beauty, light freckles, grey-green eyes, dark auburn hair loosely pinned back with a few strands falling free, natural skin with fine texture

OUTFIT · Wool and Canvas v2

FRAME: portrait
NOTE:

Caption: She had her answer ready before the boat touched the dock.

QUALITY · Bright Overcast Morning
```
medium-format film photograph, bright soft overcast light, gentle wind, clean natural colour with cool sea tones, fine film grain, crisp detail in skin, wool and rope
```

SUBJECT · The Keeper's Daughter v2
```
a woman in her late twenties with a calm, weathered beauty, light freckles, grey-green eyes, dark auburn hair loose and blown across her face by the sea wind, natural skin with fine texture, a quiet, certain expression with the trace of a smile
```

OUTFIT · Coat for the Crossing

a long belted dark navy wool coat over the cream sweater, a knitted scarf, a small leather travel case at her feet
```

POSE · Turning, Envelope in Hand

```

SCENE · Wooden Landing Stage
```
a weathered wooden landing stage at the foot of the cliff, coiled rope and iron rings, a small supply boat approaching across grey water, the lighthouse high on the cape behind her
```

CAMERA · Medium Close-Up, Eye Level

portrait medium close-up at her eye level with an 85mm lens at f/2, sharp focus on her eyes, the boat and the cliff soft in the background
